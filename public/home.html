2 · Onboarded

Won, signed and set up the same afternoon.

The engagement letter goes out for e-signature, fingerprinted. The due diligence file builds as you go: IDs, beneficial owners, screening against the EU sanctions list, and a risk rating scored the way ICPAC sets out. The client signs and sends documents from one link: no login, no attachments lost in email. Then the books open with the company’s VAT quarters, year end and deadlines.

3 · Deadlines

The deadlines write themselves.

Every client’s VAT returns, VIES statements, provisional tax and company tax return are worked out from their own VAT quarters and tax year, each with the person who looks after it. File the VAT return and its deadline closes itself; anything that slips goes to the top, and onto that client’s home screen.

The practice calendar is the work list. Nothing lives in a spreadsheet on someone’s desktop.

4 · Work

Everyone’s week on one screen. Every hour on a client.

Start the clock on any client from the top bar of any page; it keeps running while you move around. Each person’s week sits against the hours they have, the client time picked out, so you see who is overloaded on Monday, not on deadline day.

5 · Books

The shoebox, sorted.

A month of PDFs, scans and phone photos, for every client, in one drop. Read in Greek or English, checked to the cent, routed by the VAT number on the invoice, drafted as a bill.

  • The bank matches itself. Import the statement and press Match all: 18 of 20 lines, gone.
  • Full double entry. Every company in its own ledger, with a lock date the database enforces.
  • Nothing posts on its own. Sure documents go in one press, after a person says so.

6 · Filed

The VAT 4, filled in before you open it.

Worked out from the ledger, box by box, with the reverse charge in 1, 4, 7 and 11B and a VIES statement that agrees with 8B to the cent. The client approves it with one press before you file through Tax For All. Mark it filed and the quarter locks.

  • Every EU VAT number, confirmed. Each client’s customers and suppliers in other member states, checked live against VIES in one press. Every answer is kept on the audit trail as evidence.
  • A wrong digit never reaches an invoice. Check digits for all 27 states are tested as the number is typed.

The house rules

Balanced, or it doesn’t post.

The rules an auditor cares about aren’t left to good habits. The system keeps them.

RuleHow it’s kept
Every posting balancesAnything that doesn’t is refused by the database. Each company has its own ledger, and a document can only post to its own company.
A locked period stays lockedChecked on every posting, from any screen, import or API call. Corrections go in the next open period.
Every change is on the recordPostings, matches, locks and filings are chained by SHA-256 hashes, with who did each one. The chain is re-checked whenever it is opened.
